## Description
Code Timer is a clinical reference and memory aid built by a nurse for nurses and other licensed healthcare professionals.

CODE TIMER (BLS / ACLS / PALS)
• 2-minute CPR cycle with a warning at 15 seconds and an alarm for pulse/rhythm checks
• Epinephrine timer: due at 3 minutes, overdue at 5
• Shock, amiodarone and lidocaine tracking
• Metronome at 110/min
• H's and T's checklist
• Timestamped event log in military time that you can copy into your charting
• Alarms sound even with the silent switch on, and the screen stays awake during a code

PALS AND PEDIATRIC DOSING
• Enter weight once in kg or lb
• PALS arrest doses and defibrillation energy calculated from weight
• Acetaminophen and ibuprofen in mg, mL, teaspoons and tablets
• Ondansetron, dexamethasone, diphenhydramine and IM epinephrine for anaphylaxis

IV MIXING CARDS
• How to mix, how fast to run, and whether it can be pushed or given IM
• Search by drug or brand

CALCULATORS
• Hemodynamics: SVR, PVR, cardiac index, cardiac power output, PAPi, and the likely shock pattern
• ABG interpreter, step by step: Winter's formula, albumin-corrected anion gap, delta ratio, P/F and A-a gradient
• Ventilator: ideal body weight, tidal volume by mL/kg, starting settings, and what to change based on the gas

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no tracking, no ads. Nothing you enter leaves your phone.

IMPORTANT
Code Timer is not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat, and it doesn't replace clinical judgment, provider orders, pharmacy or facility protocol. Check every dose against the order and the product in hand. Sources are listed in the app under Ref → About & sources.

## Notes for the reviewer (App Review Information → Notes)
```
Code Timer is a clinical reference and memory aid for licensed healthcare
professionals, built by a registered nurse. No login is needed.

How to test:
- Code tab: tap START CODE. The CPR cycle alarm fires at 2:00; tap
  "Epi 1 mg given" to start the epinephrine timer (due at 3:00).
- Switch to PALS and enter a weight (e.g. 18 kg) to see weight-based doses.
- Calc, Vent and IV tabs work without any setup.

Accuracy and safety:
- Every dose shows its mg/kg and maximum next to the result, with caps at
  standard adult maximums. Sources (AHA, AAP, ARDSNet, FDA labeling) are in
  the app under Ref -> About & sources.
- A one-time disclaimer on first launch states it is not a medical device,
  does not replace clinical judgment, provider orders or protocol, and that
  users should check with a physician before making medical decisions.
- No data is collected or transmitted.

We understand guideline 1.4.2 applies to dosage calculators. If any part of
the app can't be approved as-is, please tell us which screens, and we will
remove or change them and resubmit.
```
