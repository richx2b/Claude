ACLS — adult cardiac arrest

Epinephrine1 mg IV/IO every 3–5 min. Non-shockable: give ASAP. Shockable: after initial shocks fail (typically after 2nd shock).
Amiodarone1st: 300 mg bolus · 2nd: 150 mg
Lidocaine1st: 1–1.5 mg/kg · then 0.5–0.75 mg/kg · max 3 mg/kg
Defib, biphasicPer manufacturer (often 120–200 J). Unknown: use max.
